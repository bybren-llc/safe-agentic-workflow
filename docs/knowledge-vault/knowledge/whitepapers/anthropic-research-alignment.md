## What it is, and is not

It is an academic-style whitepaper: it argues that specific published engineering findings are
already reflected in how this harness is built, and it reasons rather than instructs. It is not
operational guidance. Nothing in it tells you what to run, and no procedure elsewhere in the
harness depends on it. Its audience is someone deciding whether the design is principled, not
someone executing against it.

## When it earns its length

Reach for it when you are justifying the harness to a skeptic, adapting it to a context its
authors did not anticipate, or deciding whether a proposed change contradicts the reasoning the
design rests on. For all three, the argument is the product and a summary would defeat it — which
is exactly why this card does not attempt one.
